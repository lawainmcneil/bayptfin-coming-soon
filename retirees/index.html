Retired? Plan how your savings become income.

What we hear

Questions retirees bring us

Where should this month’s income come from?

Different accounts are taxed differently. The order you draw from them may affect your tax bill, and how long your money lasts depends on many factors, including spending and market conditions.

Will my income hold up if markets fall?

That depends on how your income is built. We look at which expenses are essential, which are flexible, and what covers each.

What happens to my spouse if I go first?

Some income, such as a pension or a Social Security benefit, may stop or shrink when one spouse dies. It helps to look at that early, while your options are open.

What are required minimum distributions going to do to my taxes?

Required withdrawals may raise your taxable income. Planning ahead can give you more choices.

How do I plan for healthcare and long-term care costs?

Medicare covers some costs and not others. We help you think through what could come next and how you’d pay for it.

Ongoing

Staying in touch

A plan is only useful if it keeps up with you. We revisit it with you at least once a year, and when something changes: a market drop, a health event, a move, a new rule.
