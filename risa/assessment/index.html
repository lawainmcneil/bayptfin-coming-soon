Your Income Style (RISA®)

Start with a RISA® assessment

About fifteen minutes. You’ll see your income style, and we’ll walk through what it means for your plan.

Take the assessment
How it works

Three steps

  1. 1

    Take the assessment

    About fifteen minutes, answering questions about how you feel about safety, growth, control and commitment, on RISA’s secure platform.

    Take the assessment
  2. 2

    See your results

    You’ll get your income style in plain English: what it tends to favor, and what it asks you to give up.

  3. 3

    Talk it through with us

    We go over whether it sounds like you, then use it as the starting point for your plan.

What you’ll get

  • Your retirement income style, in plain English.
  • What that style tends to favor, and what it asks you to give up.
  • A short list of questions to think about before we meet.

What RISA will ask for

RISA’s secure platform will ask for your name and email to create your profile and show you your results. We don’t ask for account numbers or Social Security numbers, and you should never enter them anywhere online for this assessment.

What happens next

Because you’re using our invitation link, we’ll see your results too. A member of our team will reach out to schedule a conversation, or you can schedule one yourself whenever you’re ready.
