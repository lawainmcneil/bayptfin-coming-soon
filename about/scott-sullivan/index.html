Meet Scott Sullivan

Scott Sullivan, CLU®, RICP®. Partner and Financial Planning Advisor.

About Scott

Scott has called New Hampshire home his entire life. Born and raised in Laconia, he grew up as the younger (and, in his words, better-looking) half of identical twins.

After graduating from the University of New Hampshire in 1991, Scott began his career in financial services. He spent the next ten years working with two national life insurance companies, helping individuals and families navigate important financial decisions.

In 2001, Scott joined Bay Point Financial, LLC, giving him the opportunity to take a more comprehensive approach to financial planning. Today, he works closely with individuals and small business owners, with a particular focus on retirement and estate planning. For Scott, the most rewarding part of the job is getting to know his clients, understanding what matters most to them, and helping them build a plan for the future.

Outside the office

Outside of work, you’ll find him on the golf course or lake in the summer, or on the ski slopes in the winter. He lives in Gilford with his wife, Allison, and they have two grown children, Hannah and Connor.

After all these years, New Hampshire is still home, and that’s exactly the way he likes it.

Designations

Chartered Life Underwriter® (CLU®) and Retirement Income Certified Professional® (RICP®), both current.
