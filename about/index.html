Why we work this way

Retirement is different from saving for it, and many people only do it once. We start with listening, because two people with the same savings can want very different things. We put the plan in writing, and we ask you to sleep on it.

Our story

Bay Point Financial was founded in 1999 by John Strasburger. Scott Sullivan joined the firm in 2001, working alongside John for more than two decades before John retired in 2025. Scott continues the firm’s work today from Laconia and Bedford.
